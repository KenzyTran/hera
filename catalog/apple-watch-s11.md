# Apple Watch Series 11

## Overview
The Apple Watch Series 11 is Apple's flagship smartwatch with the new S11 SiP, an Always-On Retina LTPO3 display, and an aerospace-grade titanium option, designed for users who want the most advanced health, fitness, and connectivity features on the wrist.

## Specifications
- Chip: S11 SiP with 64-bit dual-core processor and 4-core Neural Engine
- Memory: 64 GB internal storage
- Display: Always-On Retina LTPO3 OLED, up to 2000 nits, 1.92-inch (42mm case) or 2.05-inch (46mm case)
- Case sizes: 42mm and 46mm
- Battery: up to 36 hours typical use, up to 72 hours in Low Power Mode
- Connectivity: GPS or GPS + Cellular variants, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, Ultra-Wideband
- Materials: Aluminum (Jet Black, Rose Gold, Silver) and Titanium (Natural, Gold, Slate)
- Sensors: third-generation optical heart sensor, electrical heart sensor, blood oxygen sensor, skin temperature sensor, depth gauge
- Water resistance: WR50 plus IP6X dust resistance
- Weight: 30 grams (42mm Aluminum), 40 grams (46mm Titanium)
- Dimensions: 42 x 36 x 9.7 mm (42mm case), 46 x 39 x 9.7 mm (46mm case)
