# MacBook Pro M4

## Overview
The MacBook Pro M4 is Apple's professional notebook, available in 14-inch and 16-inch Liquid Retina XDR display sizes with M4, M4 Pro, and M4 Max chip options, built for developers, creators, and power users who need sustained performance and long battery life.

## Specifications
- Chip options: Apple M4 (10-core CPU, 10-core GPU), M4 Pro (12-core CPU, 16-core GPU), M4 Max (16-core CPU, 40-core GPU)
- Memory: 16 GB unified memory standard, configurable up to 64 GB on M4 Pro and up to 128 GB on M4 Max
- Storage options: 512 GB, 1 TB, 2 TB, 4 TB, 8 TB SSD
- Display: 14.2-inch or 16.2-inch Liquid Retina XDR with ProMotion (120 Hz adaptive refresh)
- Battery: up to 24 hours of video playback on the 16-inch model, up to 22 hours on the 14-inch model
- Colors: Space Black, Silver
- Weight: 1.55 kg (14-inch), 2.15 kg (16-inch)
- Dimensions: 312.6 x 221.2 x 15.5 mm (14-inch), 355.7 x 248.1 x 16.8 mm (16-inch)
- Ports: three Thunderbolt 5 ports, MagSafe 3, HDMI, SDXC card slot, 3.5 mm headphone jack
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.3
