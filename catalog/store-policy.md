# Store Policy

## Returns
Customers may return most items purchased from the Apple Store within 14 calendar days of receipt for a refund to the original payment method. Items must be returned in their original packaging with all accessories, manuals, and documentation. Opened iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac devices are eligible for return within the 14-day window with no restocking fee, provided the device is in original condition. Engraved or personalized products are non-refundable except in the case of manufacturing defects. Accessory-only returns (cases, cables, chargers, AirPods opened) follow the same 14-day window and must be returned in original sealed condition for a full refund; opened accessories may be subject to a partial refund at staff discretion. Refunds are typically processed within 5 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

## Store Hours
The Apple Store is open Monday through Friday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Saturday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and Sunday from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM local store time. The store is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve the store closes at 6:00 PM. Holiday hours during the December shopping season may be extended; current hours are always posted on the store locator page.

## Warranty
Every new Apple product includes a one-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects and 90 days of complimentary technical support from the original date of purchase. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use; it does not cover accidental damage, unauthorized modifications, or damage caused by liquid contact unless covered by an extended plan. AppleCare+ is available as an optional add-on purchased within 60 days of the device purchase date and extends hardware coverage to two years (three years for Mac), adds unlimited incidents of accidental damage protection (subject to a service fee per incident), and includes 24/7 priority technical support. AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss is available for iPhone in supported regions. Warranty service can be initiated in-store, by phone, or online through the Apple Support portal.

## Contact
Customers may reach the store by phone at the local store number listed on the Apple store locator, by email through the Apple Support contact form, or in person during published store hours. In-store pickup is available for online orders typically within two hours of order confirmation, subject to inventory; customers receive an email or text notification when the order is ready for pickup. For technical support outside of store hours, Apple Support is available 24 hours a day through chat and phone via the Apple Support app and the support website.
